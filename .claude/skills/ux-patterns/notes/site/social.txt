### GENUINELY EXTRA — not a title or an obvious synonym of any of the 76 slugs:
  1. "Fitt's law applied to design" — Fitts's Law (target size vs. distance trades off
     against speed/accuracy of pointing) is not one of the 76 pattern titles at all
     (closest neighbors — golden-ratio, proximity-rule, gestalt-laws, von-restorff,
     serial-position, peak-end-rule, doherty-threshold, zeigarnik-effect — cover other
     named psychology/UX laws but not this one). Directly relevant to Kladra: click
     targets on dense tables/kanban cards, icon-only action buttons, and touch targets on
     tablet use in the yard should be sized/spaced by Fitts's Law, not just by the type
     scale. See deeper.txt for the authoritative NN/g source and concrete numbers.
  2. "Kanban UX tips" — no kanban-specific slug exists among the 76 (closest is the
     general drag-and-drop pattern). Kladra's own project/dispatch board is exactly a
     kanban surface, so this is a direct gap worth flagging even though the specific tip
     content of the short itself could not be read (captions only, not video/transcript).
  3. "Card input IX tips" (sic, likely "UX tips") — ambiguous: could mean payment/credit-
     card number input UX, or "card" as in the perfect-card-style content card. Neither
     matches the 76 slugs (input-masking is the closest neighbor for formatted-input UX).
     Flagged as extra but low-confidence on exact content since only the caption, not the
     video, was reachable.
