TITLE: Color Picker UX
CATEGORY: interaction
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/color-picker-ux

PROBLEM: A bare gradient-and-slider color picker treats color choice as decoration when every pick actually cascades into contrast, transparency, and palette decisions across the whole UI.

RULES:
- Treat the picker as a decision tool, not a gradient with a slider — every choice cascades into the rest of the UI.
- Offer OKLCH next to hex: hex is for machines, OKLCH's lightness/chroma/hue lets a person change one number and get a predictable shade (video shows `rgb(59,130,246)` vs `oklch(0.74 0.19 259)` with sliders for each of L/C/H).
- Give the picker memory: recent swatches and saved palettes put the last five picks one tap away instead of re-hunting.
- Show a live contrast ratio at pick time, not in review — a badge that flips red (FAIL) to green (PASS) kills failing pairs before they ship (video: "2.5:1 FAIL" vs "4.8:1 AA" badges live next to the swatch).
- Preview alpha over a checkerboard on both light and dark backgrounds — transparency lies on a plain white canvas.
- Turn one pick into a system: generate tints/shades from a single hue to produce ten tokens (blue-50 through blue-900) from one decision.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: expose human-readable formats like OKLCH so one value maps to a predictable shade.
- Do: surface recent swatches and saved palettes so past picks stay one tap away.
- Do: validate contrast live while picking, with a red/green badge.
- Don't: preview alpha only on white — use a checkerboard to reveal true transparency.
- Don't: ship a bare gradient-and-slider picker with no memory, contrast check, or palette output.

KLADRA: Most relevant if Kladra ever exposes a color picker to an end user (e.g. a tag/label color for lead status, a dispatch calendar color) rather than only using a closed DESIGN.md token palette. Given CLAUDE.md's "Five files, nothing else" and a fixed design-token system with "exactly two gradients," Kladra likely does NOT want an open-ended color picker in the product at all — the live-contrast-badge and OKLCH-over-hex ideas are the parts worth keeping if any picker exists (e.g. an admin-only settings screen), while the "memory/recent swatches" and "saved palettes" features risk drifting toward a personalization feature Kladra hasn't asked for. Flag as likely LOW APPLICABILITY unless SPEC/DESIGN calls for user-chosen colors anywhere.

PRIORITY: low — Kladra's fixed, pre-defined design-token approach (not user-customizable color) makes a full color-picker component unlikely to be needed; keep only the live-contrast-check idea in mind if any settings screen ever exposes color choice.
