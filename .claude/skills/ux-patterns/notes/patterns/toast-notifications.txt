TITLE: Toast Notifications
CATEGORY: Feedback
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/toast-notifications

PROBLEM: Toasts are meant to inform without interrupting, but sloppy placement, timing, stacking or color choices turn them into something that blocks work, disappears too fast for a real problem, or is unreadable to colorblind users.

RULES:
- Position deliberately: bottom-right on desktop, top edge on mobile. Screen center is explicitly off-limits — sheet captions it "center · blocks content" and the on-screen line reads "Center blocks what users are actually doing," because a centered toast covers the very content the user is working on and intercepts clicks.
- Match dismiss timing to severity: routine info auto-dismisses in ~4s (sheet shows "File uploaded" example labeled "4S · AUTO-DISMISS"), warnings hold ~7s ("Session expiring" labeled "7S · HOLDS LONGER"), and critical errors ("Payment failed") stay until the user acknowledges them, labeled "UNTIL ACKNOWLEDGED."
- Cap the visible stack at 3 toasts. Sheet shows the exact spring used to shuffle them: damping 20, stiffness 180, captioned "MAX 3 VISIBLE." Newest enters at the bottom, older ones float upward and out (or queue) rather than piling up indefinitely.
- Always give a way out: a close (x) button on desktop, swipe-to-dismiss on mobile, and hover-to-pause so the countdown timer freezes while the user is reading (sheet shows all three labeled "CLICK," "SWIPE," "HOVER" against their respective actions: "Always offer a way out," "Gesture on mobile," "Timer freezes until you leave").
- Color-code by type (info/blue, success/green, warning/yellow, error/red) but never rely on color alone — pair every toast with a matching icon and a left accent border, because the sheet cites "6% of users can't tell them apart" (colorblindness) as the reason.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: anchor toasts bottom-right on desktop, top edge on mobile.
- Do: scale dismiss time to severity — ~4s info, ~7s warning, hold errors until acknowledged.
- Do: cap the stack at 3 visible toasts with spring-based reflow (damping 20 / stiffness 180), queuing the rest.
- Do: pause the auto-dismiss timer on hover.
- Do: give every toast type a distinct icon and accent-border color, not color alone.
- Don't: place toasts center-screen, where they block active content and clicks.
- Don't: auto-dismiss critical errors — hold until acknowledged.
- Don't: show more than 3 toasts at once without queuing.
