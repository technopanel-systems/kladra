TITLE: Card Hover Anatomy
CATEGORY: motion
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/card-hover-anatomy

PROBLEM: The same card can feel alive or dead on hover depending on four specific motion rules — most hover treatments break at least one of them.

RULES:
- Lift with weight: raise the card ~8px on hover and stretch its shadow with it, over ~200ms ease-out. Faster reads as twitchy, slower feels stuck (video shows exactly this 3-way comparison: fast = "twitchy," 200ms = correct, slow = "stuck").
- Claim the cursor: a pulsing accent border or a gradient sweep around the edge signals interactivity instead of leaving the card visually flat.
- Cascade the actions: reveal hidden action buttons (favorite/cart/share) staggered ~60ms apart, anchored at the bottom — a reveal should add an affordance, not shift layout.
- Push against the glass: scale the inner image to ~1.05 inside an `overflow-hidden` frame while the outer container stays fixed size — the product presses outward instead of the whole card resizing.
- The trap: never scale the whole card — that shifts neighboring cards and breaks the grid. Animate the content, keep the footprint (geometry) fixed.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: lift the card ~8px and grow its shadow together over ~200ms ease-out.
- Do: stagger revealed action buttons ~60ms apart, anchored to the bottom edge.
- Do: scale the image ~1.05 inside an overflow-hidden frame while the container holds still.
- Don't: scale the entire card — it shifts neighbors and breaks the grid.
- Don't: stack action buttons over the title or let them spill outside the card boundary.
- Don't: time the lift too fast (twitchy) or too slow (stuck).

KLADRA: Applies to any card-grid view Kladra has — companies/projects board views, dashboard summary cards, the coordinator's queue cards. The lift+shadow+stagger recipe gives concrete numbers (8px, 200ms, 60ms) that fit inside Kladra's stated 100ms hover budget only partially: the 200ms lift is slower than Kladra's "100ms for hover/menus" rule — a CONFLICT worth flagging plainly, since this pattern's signature "weight" feel depends on the slower 200ms timing. Recommend keeping the lift fast (closer to 100–150ms) to stay in Kladra's budget, accepting a slightly less "weighty" feel, rather than adopting 200ms verbatim. The "claim the cursor" gradient-sweep border risks conflicting with "exactly two gradients (primary action, logo mark)" if applied to every card — use a plain accent-color border/ring instead of a gradient sweep on hover to stay within that budget. The geometry-preservation rule (never scale the whole card) is a clean, conflict-free correctness rule Kladra should keep regardless.

PRIORITY: medium — useful hover vocabulary for board/dashboard cards, but two of its four signature moves (200ms timing, gradient-sweep border) need trimming to fit Kladra's motion-timing and two-gradients rules.
