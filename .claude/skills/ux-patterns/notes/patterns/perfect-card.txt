TITLE: Perfect Card
CATEGORY: Visual
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/perfect-card

PROBLEM: Two cards with identical content can look "free" (unstyled default) or "premium" (deliberate) depending on four small CSS decisions, not on the content itself.

RULES:
- Padding is the biggest tell: going from a cramped ~12px to ~40px, paired with a 24px border-radius, instantly reads as intentional instead of cheap. Sheet's basic card is tight and square-cornered; the premium card labels its padding explicitly and pairs it with a visibly rounder corner.
- Build a real type hierarchy — push the title to 600 weight and roughly 38px, then shrink the body copy and drop it to ~55% opacity so the eye lands on the title first. Sheet shows the title/body pairing with `font-weight` and an opacity value called out directly on the frame.
- Stack two shadows for believable depth: a tight, darker shadow for contrast plus a wide, soft shadow for ambient elevation — one shadow alone reads flat or artificial.
- Add a hairline border at roughly 12% opacity to define the card's edge against a dark background, rather than relying on the shadow alone to separate it from the page.
- Give the card a hover state — lift it about 8px, scale to roughly 1.02, and deepen the shadow — so it visibly signals "clickable" and adds the final layer of polish. Sheet shows a cursor hovering the card with the lift/scale/shadow change applied live.
- Same content, four changes: spacing (padding/radius), typography (weight/size/opacity), shadows (layered), and hover are the entire gap between a card that looks free and one that looks like it cost real design effort.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: layer a tight shadow for contrast with a soft ambient one for depth, plus a subtle border around 12% opacity.
- Do: set the description to ~55% opacity so the title clearly wins the hierarchy.
- Do: give the card a hover lift (~8px up, slight scale, deeper shadow) so it feels interactive.
- Don't: cram content against the edges with tiny padding and near-zero border-radius — it reads as an unstyled default.
- Don't: give the title and body the same weight and full opacity, so nothing guides the eye.
