TITLE: Animation Timing
CATEGORY: motion
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/animation-timing

PROBLEM: The same UI feels premium or broken purely based on how many milliseconds its motion takes and which easing curve it uses.

RULES:
- Entrances: 200–300ms with cubic ease-out — fast enough to feel responsive, slow enough to read as deliberate. The video marks 250ms as "perfect", 50ms as "too fast", 700ms as "too slow".
- Exits should run faster than entrances — pair a 250ms entrance with a ~150ms exit (video states exits are ~30-40% faster: "250 → 150", "Exits are 30% faster. The user already decided.").
- Tap/press feedback must land under 100ms or it reads as lag even when the action is instant. Video shows a 3-way comparison: 200ms = "laggy", 100ms = "OK", 50ms = "instant", captioned "<100ms — Press. Hover. Toggle. The Doherty threshold — feels instant."
- Attention-grabbing motion (notifications, alerts) can run longer and use bounce/overshoot: 500–800ms.
- Stagger list items ~50ms apart. Video shows 30ms = "too fast" (items blur together), 50ms = "perfect", 100ms = "too slow" (list crawls in).
- Match easing to intent: ease-out for entrances; reserve spring/bounce for moments that must grab attention.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: use ease-out curves for entrances so motion decelerates into place.
- Do: make exits roughly 30-40% faster than their matching entrance.
- Do: keep tap/press feedback under 100ms.
- Don't: stretch entrances past ~300ms.
- Don't: use symmetric in/out timing.
- Don't: use linear easing on entrances — reads mechanical and cheap.

VIDEO ADDS: Every number in the text is also shown as an on-screen stat with a labeled demo: a confirm-delete modal at 500ms (feels sluggish) vs 250ms (feels right); a "Saved" toast that fires correctly inside a 250ms-in/150ms-out modal vs one where the modal takes 250ms in but a mismatched 250ms out ("Some spend + sluggish exit"); three color-coded buttons (red/orange/green) demonstrating 200ms/100ms/50ms feedback lag explicitly tied to "the Doherty threshold"; a settings-list stagger demo run at 30/50/100ms per item.
