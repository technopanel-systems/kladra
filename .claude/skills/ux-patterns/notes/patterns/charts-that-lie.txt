TITLE: Charts That Lie
CATEGORY: visual
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/charts-that-lie

PROBLEM: The same underlying data can tell opposite stories depending purely on how the chart is drawn — axis baseline, chart type, aspect ratio, and color choice all bias the reading.

RULES:
- Start every bar chart's y-axis at zero, no exceptions. Truncating the baseline turns a real +4% change into a visual "+400%"-looking explosion — the single most common way charts mislead (video: two identical 47%-vs-48% bars, one truncated axis reading "Landslide?", one zero-based reading "or a tie").
- Match chart type to the question: bars compare values, lines show change over time, pie charts fall apart past ~5 slices. The question should pick the form, not taste.
- Aspect ratio rewrites the trend — the same rising series looks flat when squished ("Flat?") or like a spike when stretched ("Spike?"); balance it so the average slope sits near 45° to read honestly.
- Maximize the data-ink ratio: strip gridlines, drop shadows, 3D skew, boxed legends; label the line directly. Video shows a literal "data-ink" meter jumping from 45% to 84% after chartjunk removal.
- Color is encoding, not decoration — use one hero/accent color to spotlight the series that matters; choose categorical, sequential, or diverging palettes to match the data type (three example swatch scales shown).
- Title the chart with the takeaway, not the metric: "Revenue flat since March" beats "Quarterly revenue."

DO / DON'T:
- Do: start every bar chart's y-axis at zero.
- Do: pick chart type from the question being answered.
- Do: spotlight the one series that matters with a single accent color.
- Don't: truncate or crop an axis to exaggerate small differences.
- Don't: add gridlines, shadows, or 3D effects that encode no data.
- Don't: use a pie chart with more than five slices.

KLADRA: Lands squarely on Kladra's own rule, P13: "no chart for decoration: a bar, a pie or a ring only where it answers a question a manager asks." This pattern is the execution discipline behind that rule for Kladra's dashboards and reports (Abdulrahman's manager view, the report builder, metrics screens) — zero-based bars, chart-type-matches-question, and title-as-takeaway should be checked against every chart Kladra ships. The one-hero-color rule also reinforces "no chart for decoration": if a chart needs more than one accent color to make its point, it's probably decorating rather than answering a question. No conflict with any standing decision — pure chart-honesty discipline. Diverging/sequential palette needs to stay inside Kladra's dark-mode-by-default, WCAG-contrast rules (see color-accessibility.txt).
