TITLE: Accordion Disclosure
CATEGORY: interaction
URL: https://designmotionhq.com/patterns/accordion-disclosure

PROBLEM: Expand/collapse panels are usually built with a naive height transition or a plain div, so they either snap open instead of animating or fail keyboard and screen-reader users entirely.

RULES:
- `height: auto` cannot be transitioned — it snaps. Either use `display: grid` with `grid-template-rows` animating 0fr -> 1fr, or measure `scrollHeight` and transition to that pixel value.
- The demo's own CSS sample uses `display: grid; grid-template-rows: 1fr; transition: 0.3s;` — i.e. a ~300ms panel transition.
- The chevron icon must rotate on the exact same timing curve as the panel height change; the video calls out that even ~10 frames of lag between chevron and panel reads as broken, not smooth.
- Decide up front: accordion (one panel open at a time) vs disclosure (many can stay open). Sequential/step content should stay single-open; FAQ-style lists should allow multi-open.
- The clickable header must be a real `<button>`, not a `<div>`, with `aria-expanded` reflecting open/closed state and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel id — this is what makes Enter/Space toggle it and gives it a visible focus ring.
- When a lower item in the list expands, anchor the tapped header's scroll position so the page doesn't jump, and stagger the newly revealed content in rather than showing it instantly.

DO / DON'T:
- Do: drive chevron rotation and panel height off one shared timing curve so they move as a unit.
- Do: render the header as a real `<button>` wired with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- Do: match open behavior to content type — one-at-a-time for steps, many-open for FAQs.
- Don't: animate `height: auto` directly — use grid-rows or a measured pixel value instead.
- Don't: let the chevron trail the panel by even a few frames.
- Don't: let the list scroll-jump when a lower item expands — keep the tapped header anchored.

KLADRA: Applies to any collapsible group — company/project record drawer sections (contact info, documents, activity), FAQ-style help content, filter-chip groups that expand for more options, and mobile-width card lists where a row expands into details instead of opening a full drawer. The single-vs-multi-open decision matters for the coordinator's queue (each request likely single-open, since only one should be "active" at a time) versus a settings-style FAQ. No conflict with any standing decision — grid-rows/scrollHeight technique and button semantics are pure implementation hygiene. The suggested ~300ms panel duration sits above Kladra's 100ms hover/menu budget but fits inside the 150–250ms dialog/drawer band loosely (slightly over); use 200–250ms to stay in-budget rather than the demo's 300ms.

PRIORITY: high — accordions/disclosures are a near-certain fit for record drawers and FAQ/help content, and the aria-expanded/aria-controls + grid-rows technique is exactly the kind of correctness bug Kladra should not ship.
